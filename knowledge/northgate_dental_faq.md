# Northgate Family Dental - patient FAQ

Northgate Family Dental is a fictional clinic used for this demo. All names, prices and contact details are made up.

Q: Where are you located?
A: 214 Northgate Avenue, Suite 3, above the pharmacy. Free parking for patients is behind the building, entrance on Elm Street. The clinic is step-free and has a lift.

Q: What are your opening hours?
A: Monday to Thursday 8:00 to 18:00, Friday 8:00 to 15:00, and the first Saturday of each month 9:00 to 13:00. Closed on Sundays and public holidays.

Q: How do I book an appointment?
A: Leave your name, email and what you need with our website assistant, or call the front desk on (555) 014-2290 during opening hours. The team confirms a time by email within one business day. Online booking of exact time slots is not available yet.

Q: Are you taking new patients?
A: Yes, adults and children. A first visit includes a full check-up, X-rays if needed and a written treatment plan with prices.

Q: How much does a check-up cost?
A: A new patient exam with X-rays is $95. A regular check-up for existing patients is $60. A scale and polish (hygienist cleaning) is $85.

Q: How much does teeth whitening cost?
A: In-clinic whitening is $390 and takes about 90 minutes. A take-home whitening kit with custom trays is $240. Whitening is only done after a check-up, so the dentist can confirm it is safe for your teeth.

Q: Do you offer clear aligners?
A: Yes. Clear aligner treatment starts with a $150 consultation with 3D scan. The full treatment usually costs between $3,200 and $4,800 depending on the case, and the exact price is in the written plan after the consultation.

Q: Do you do dental implants?
A: Yes. A single implant including the crown usually costs $2,900 to $3,400. An implant consultation is $120 and is deducted from the treatment price if you go ahead.

Q: Which insurance do you accept?
A: We accept most PPO dental plans and send claims for you. We are not in-network with HMO plans. Bring your insurance card to your first visit and the front desk checks your coverage before treatment.

Q: Do you offer payment plans?
A: Yes. Treatments over $500 can be split into 6 or 12 monthly payments with no interest, subject to approval. We accept cash, debit and credit cards. We do not accept cheques.

Q: Do you treat children?
A: Yes, from age 2. Children's check-ups are $45. We recommend a first visit by the child's second birthday.

Q: What should I do in a dental emergency, such as a broken or knocked-out tooth, bad toothache or swelling?
A: Call (555) 014-2290. We keep same-day emergency slots every weekday morning. An emergency visit is $75 plus any treatment. Outside opening hours, the voicemail gives the number of the on-call dentist. For heavy bleeding or swelling that affects breathing, go to the nearest emergency room.

Q: What is your cancellation policy?
A: Please cancel or move an appointment at least 24 hours before. A late cancellation or missed appointment is charged $40. The first one is waived.

Q: Do you offer sedation for nervous patients?
A: Yes. We offer nitrous oxide ("laughing gas") for $65 per visit. Tell us when you book that you are nervous and we will plan a longer, slower first visit.

Q: Who are the dentists?
A: Dr. Amira Haddad (general dentistry and implants), Dr. Lucas Brenner (clear aligners and cosmetic work) and hygienist Joanne Park.

Q: How can I contact the clinic?
A: Phone (555) 014-2290 during opening hours, or email hello@northgatedental.example. Replies to email are sent within one business day.
